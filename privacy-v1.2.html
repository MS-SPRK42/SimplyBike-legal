SimplyBike

Privacy Policy

SimplyBike is designed to be a simple, low-pressure riding companion. SimplyBike is provided by SPRK42 LTD, operating on Google Play under the developer name SPRK42.

What we collect

• Email address for account access
• Account preferences and profile information
• Ride data such as duration, intervals, optional distance, and optional feedback
• Equipment settings, including resistance range
• Subscription and purchase-verification information needed to confirm Google Play purchases
• App usage, diagnostic and crash information used to understand app performance and reliability

What we do with it

• Save your rides so you can view your progress
• Maintain your account, equipment settings and preferences
• Verify and maintain subscription access
• Improve app reliability, stability and performance
• Diagnose crashes and technical problems

What we do not do

• We do not sell your personal data
• We do not use your personal data for advertising
• We do not track you across unrelated apps or services for advertising purposes

Services that process information

SimplyBike uses services provided by Google and Firebase to operate the app. These include Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Google Play Billing.

These services may process information on our behalf for purposes including account authentication, cloud storage, subscription verification, app usage measurement, crash reporting and technical diagnostics.

Storage and security

SimplyBike account and ride data is stored using Firebase and Google Cloud services. We use technical and access controls intended to protect account information and restrict access to authorised app and server processes.

Data retention

Your SimplyBike account information, saved rides, equipment settings, feedback and subscription records are retained while your account remains active, unless they are removed earlier through normal app use.

Diagnostic and analytics information may be retained by Google or Firebase according to the configuration and retention periods of those services.

Account and data deletion

You can delete your SimplyBike account and associated personal data from within the app at any time.

In the SimplyBike app:
From the Home screen, tap the Settings (gear) icon. Scroll to the Account Security section and select Delete Account. Follow the on-screen confirmation steps.

When account deletion completes, SimplyBike deletes your Firebase Authentication account, SimplyBike account profile and preferences, saved ride data, equipment settings, feedback associated with your account and SimplyBike subscription record.

By email:
You can also request deletion by emailing support@sprk42.com from the email address associated with your SimplyBike account. Please state that you would like your SimplyBike account and associated data deleted.

Purchase-security record retained after deletion

Following account deletion, SimplyBike retains a minimal Google Play purchase-ownership record for security and fraud-prevention purposes. This prevents a purchase that has already been associated with a SimplyBike account from being transferred or re-claimed by another account.

The retained record does not contain your SimplyBike user ID, email address, saved rides, equipment information, SimplyBike subscription record or raw Google Play purchase token.

It contains only the purchase provider, a deleted-owner status, and relevant creation, deletion and update timestamps.

This minimal record is retained for as long as necessary to protect purchase ownership and prevent reuse, transfer or fraudulent re-claiming. It is not used for advertising, profiling or activity tracking.

Your choices

You may stop using SimplyBike at any time and may delete your account using the in-app deletion controls described above.

Contact

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, contact support@sprk42.com.

Updates

This policy may be updated as SimplyBike evolves. When material changes are made, the updated policy will be published at this location.
