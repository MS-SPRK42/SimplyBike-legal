Delete Your SimplyBike Account

SimplyBike is provided by SPRK42 LTD, operating on Google Play under the developer name SPRK42.

SimplyBike allows users to permanently delete their account and associated personal data directly from within the app.

How to delete your account

1. Open SimplyBike
2. From the Home screen, tap the Settings (gear) icon
3. Scroll to the Account Security section
4. Select Delete Account
5. Follow the on-screen confirmation steps

What data is deleted

When account deletion completes, SimplyBike permanently deletes the personal account data associated with your SimplyBike account, including:

• Your Firebase Authentication account
• Your SimplyBike user profile and account preferences
• Saved ride history and ride data
• Equipment profiles and resistance settings
• Feedback associated with your account
• Your SimplyBike subscription record

What data is retained

Following account deletion, SimplyBike retains a minimal Google Play purchase-ownership safety record.

This record exists only to prevent a Google Play purchase that has already been associated with a SimplyBike account from being transferred, re-used or fraudulently re-claimed by another SimplyBike account.

The retained record does not contain your SimplyBike user ID, email address, saved rides, equipment information, SimplyBike subscription record or raw Google Play purchase token.

It contains only the purchase provider, a deleted-owner status, and relevant creation, deletion and update timestamps.

Retention period

The minimal purchase-ownership safety record is retained for as long as necessary to prevent a previously claimed Google Play purchase from being transferred, re-used or fraudulently re-claimed.

It is used only for purchase security and is not used for advertising, profiling or activity tracking.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the in-app deletion option, you can request deletion by emailing support@sprk42.com from the email address associated with your SimplyBike account.

Please state that you would like your SimplyBike account and associated data deleted.

Need help?

If you experience problems deleting your account, contact:

support@sprk42.com
